Replace this guidance with your own verified facts before saving it in Settings or
candidate_context.txt. Plain text is fine; use these topics to supply useful evidence:

Target opportunity: the role or work area you want, with the correct level (internship,
entry-level, experienced, etc.). Say whether you seek a job or a career conversation.

Current background: your accurate education or work status. Include dates, graduation
year or years of experience only when current and relevant. Do not exaggerate seniority.

Strongest relevant project: what you personally built or contributed, the actual tools
you used, and one concrete feature, tested behavior or outcome. Clearly label coursework,
personal projects, prototypes and team contributions. Include metrics only if measured.

Other evidence: one or two additional achievements or projects that may suit different
roles. Explain what each skill was used for instead of supplying an unsupported skill list.

Constraints: availability, location or work authorization only if you want them mentioned
and can state them accurately. Omit anything unknown; do not guess.

Proof: a public project/demo URL or portfolio link only when it belongs to your work and
supports the claims above. Profile links can also be saved in the separate Settings fields.

For a follow-up, explicitly date any genuine update since the original outreach. Otherwise
the AI should send a short reminder without inventing new progress.
